Video Studio Upload: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Video Studio Upload is a personal command-line tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

With your permission, it uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and caption files you choose, and to read your channel's name to confirm the right channel.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the computer that runs the tool. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can remove the tool's access at any time at Google Account permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
